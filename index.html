What We Do

Cooling things,
not buildings.

Conventional air conditioning conditions an entire building to make the people inside comfortable. Clearly Cool works differently — our chilled radiant panels are placed directly above occupied zones, absorbing heat from people and animals by radiation. The same physics that makes shade feel cool, operating 24 hours a day.

The result: targeted comfort at a fraction of the energy, with no ducts, no plumbing, and no infrastructure investment. A standard 120V outlet. 85 watts per panel. Solar-ready.

The Key Insight

Same air. Completely different comfort.

Air temperature and humidity alone don't determine how hot you feel. These four scenarios can have identical air temperature and humidity — yet produce radically different levels of thermal stress. The difference is entirely due to the radiant temperature of surrounding surfaces. This is the physical principle Clearly Cool exploits.

75°C
Direct Sunlight
Hot surfaces and direct solar radiation combine to overwhelm the body's ability to shed heat — even in a breeze.
45°C
Shade + Hot Wall
Moving into shade removes solar gain, but nearby hot masonry still radiates heat back at you. The air feels no different.
35°C
Tree Shade
Tree canopy provides cool, low-emissivity shade. Body heat now radiates outward — this is what makes shade under trees feel genuinely cooler.
25°C
Radiant Cooling Panel
A chilled overhead surface acts as an artificial cold sky — pulling heat from the body by radiation, achieving comfort levels below what tree shade alone can provide.

All four scenarios can share the same dry-bulb air temperature and relative humidity. The only variable is the mean radiant temperature — which conventional air conditioning never directly addresses, but Clearly Cool does.

The Science

Why radiation beats convection for comfort.

The human body exchanges heat with its surroundings in four ways: conduction, convection, evaporation, and radiation. Air conditioning addresses only convection — it cools the air. Radiant cooling addresses the single largest contributor to thermal comfort in many conditions: the temperature of the surfaces you're surrounded by.

When a Clearly Cool panel is placed overhead, it acts as a cold sky — absorbing radiant heat from occupants below. No air movement means no drafts, no noise, and no dry-skin discomfort. People feel cooler even when the ambient air temperature is unchanged.

Applications

Anywhere people gather in heat.

The same core technology scales from a single dining table to a full barn row or transit corridor. Radiant cooling works wherever a chilled panel can be positioned overhead — indoors or out.

Outdoor Dining & Hospitality

Extend the patio season and keep guests comfortable without blowing cold air or disrupting ambiance. Targeted cooling, zero noise.

Sports & Athletics

Dugouts, sidelines, and player recovery zones. Cool the athletes, not just their drinks.

Public Spaces & Community Events

Parks, plazas, transit shelters, and public gatherings — solar-ready panels bring cooling equity to the people who need it most.

Residential & Patio

Bring the feel of natural shade to any outdoor space, on demand. No permanent installation required.

Animal Agriculture

Targeted cooling directly over individual animals — without conditioning an entire barn. Proven in farrowing and gestation pilots.

Construction & Industrial

Worker rest stations and shaded cooling zones on job sites, powered from standard outlets or portable solar.
