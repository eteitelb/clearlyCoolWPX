About the Tool

What CoolSwine Models

CoolSwine translates barn conditions into actual thermal load on the animal — then shows you exactly how much radiant cooling changes the outcome. Built on swine-specific thermal physiology, not generic animal models.

🌡️

Thermal Load Modeling

Input air temperature, humidity, and radiant conditions. The tool calculates the effective heat stress experienced by the pig — not just ambient temperature.

The Science

Built on Peer-Reviewed Swine Research

CoolSwine isn't a rule-of-thumb calculator. It models thermal physiology the way researchers do — accounting for metabolic heat generation, radiant exchange, convection, and evaporation specific to Sus scrofa.

  • Swine-specific metabolic and thermoregulatory parameters
  • Radiant Mean Temperature (RMT) modeling — not just dry-bulb air temp
  • Validated against field measurements from summer 2025 farrowing pilots
  • Linked to 10+ academic publications from the Clearly Cool research team
  • Developed at Princeton University and AIL Research
"Conventional temperature-humidity indices were designed for humans or dairy cattle. CoolSwine applies the correct physiological model for pigs — which thermoregulate very differently, especially in farrowing." — Dr. Eric Teitelbaum, Founder & CTO, Clearly Cool
