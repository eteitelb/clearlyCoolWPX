Applications

Radiant Cooling for People & Animals

One technology platform — from farrowing rooms to beach umbrellas. Wherever heat stress limits comfort or productivity, Clearly Cool delivers targeted relief at a fraction of the energy cost.

Agriculture

🐖 Pork Production

Heat stress in farrowing and gestation costs producers in reduced litter size, lower daily gain, and impaired reproductive performance. Clearly Cool targets cooling at the animal — not the whole building.

Farrowing

Crate-Level Cooling

One panel per crate, mounted directly overhead. Cools the sow through radiant heat exchange while leaving the piglet zone unaffected.

  • No modifications to existing crate hardware
  • 85W per panel — runs off a standard outlet
  • Solar-pairable for off-grid barns
  • Silent, draftless — no stress on newborn piglets

Gestation — Cooling Stations

Covered canopy stations let sows self-select cooling as needed. Panels can integrate lighting and heating for year-round multi-function use across the barn.

Packaged Radiant Heat Pump

Self-contained unit — no external chiller. Ideal for new builds or retrofitting existing barns. Scales across rows with simple electrical hookup. Patent pending.

Hospitality

🍽️ Outdoor Dining

Extend your patio season — in summer or shoulder seasons — without propane heaters or commercial HVAC. Clearly Cool panels mount unobtrusively into pergolas, awnings, and overhead structures.

Comfort Without the Equipment

Propane patio heaters are increasingly banned in urban areas and emit CO₂. Our radiant panels provide both cooling and heating — silent, clean, and architecturally low-profile.

  • Cooling in summer, heating in cool seasons
  • No noise, no flame, no CO₂ emissions
  • Integrates into existing pergola or awning structures
  • Water-cooled translucent panel option maintains natural light
