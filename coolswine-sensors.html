Live Monitoring

Every Variable That Drives Heat Stress — In One System

The CoolSwine sensor array captures the full thermal environment around each animal, not just ambient air temperature. This is the data producers have never had before.

  • Respiratory rate (breaths per minute) — with Low / Normal / High classification
  • Core body temperature estimation
  • Air temperature
  • Relative humidity & dew point
  • Radiant temperature
  • Air speed
The Hardware

A Sensor That Lives in the Barn

The CoolSwine sensor is a compact IoT device designed for agricultural environments. It mounts at animal level — where temperature actually matters — and streams live data to the monitoring dashboard.

  • Non-contact respiratory rate detection — no animal contact required
  • Skin-surface and core temperature estimation
  • Integrated radiant temperature, air temp, humidity, and air speed sensors
  • Wireless connectivity — no barn wiring required
  • Rugged design for continuous barn operation
  • Visual status indicators (normal / elevated / high alert)
The Dashboard

At-a-Glance Heat Stress Status for Every Animal

The CoolSwine Monitor displays live readings from every sensor in the barn. Respiratory rate is classified in real time as Low, Normal, or High — so stockpeople can act immediately rather than relying on visual observation alone.

  • Live respiratory rate with Low / Normal / High zone classification
  • Core temperature trending — catch stress before it peaks
  • Full environmental context: radiant temp, humidity, dew point, air speed
  • Recent readings log for each sensor node
  • Accessible from any device — phone, tablet, or barn computer
The Full System

Sense It. Cool It. Close the Loop.

CoolSwine Sensors are designed to work hand-in-hand with Clearly Cool radiant panels — giving producers both the data to understand heat stress and the hardware to eliminate it.

  • 1
    Sensor Detects StressRespiratory rate climbs above 40 bpm, core temp rises — the dashboard flags the animal in real time.
  • 2
    Stockperson Is AlertedInstant notification on phone or barn display. No guessing, no walking every row — you know exactly which crate needs attention.
  • 3
    Radiant Panel EngagesThe Clearly Cool panel above the flagged crate can be activated manually or automatically — providing immediate, targeted cooling to that animal only.
  • 4
    Recovery Is ConfirmedThe sensor confirms respiratory rate returning to normal range. No overcooling, no guesswork — closed-loop animal welfare in action.
